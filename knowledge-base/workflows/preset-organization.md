# Organize saved presets

Resolve exact model, capacity and firmware. Retain integrity-checked complete original captures for every source, destination and chosen moved-from replacement. Keep zero-based slots distinct from display numbering. A backup is source evidence; it does not authorize a hardware write.

Preview names, destinations, overwritten originals, copied default scenes and references before execution. Derive every assignment from the immutable original pool so overlapping copies, swaps and reordering cannot consume intermediate results. Renaming owns only the preset name field and required integrity values. Whole-image assignments carry the donor's complete settings, scene names, MIDI, routing, opaque bytes and saved default scene. Do not infer an empty preset: require explicit caller-supplied replacement content for a vacated source.

Use separate destination transactions: verify fresh source/destination state and current unsaved context, load the original destination when authorized, upload once to temporary memory, compare the complete expected image, Save once, read saved contents repeatedly, reload and verify again. Different before/result default scenes require guarded alignment with complete preservation checks, not an inferred scene field exemption. Stop on stale, partial or uncertain state without replay or automatic rollback. Multi-preset changes are not atomic, and sampled comparisons cannot exclude every transient change.

Retain progress for verified completed, uncertain active and untouched pending targets. Reconcile by queries only. Undo requires complete saved/reloaded receipts and fresh current captures matching the recorded result or exact original; restore only verified eligible targets and refuse later changes. Preserve unsaved work or obtain explicit scoped discard intent separately. Already-restored originals can become no-ops; do not adopt an uncertain Save as a completed receipt.

Moving a preset does not update song, footswitch or MIDI references. Complete preset captures do not establish user-cab identity or back up global settings. Individual imports need qualified model/firmware context and transfer shape; same-model framing is not cross-model conversion or proof of asset compatibility.

Manufacturer [preset management guidance](https://support.fractalaudio.com/en-US/installing-factory-presets-1410465) documents these organization operations. Pinned Gen-3 `rawPatchName` locates the raw header name at offset 0x08 for 32 bytes across its model definitions. These are documentation/open implementation facts, separate from physical toolkit qualification.
